Executive Cost & Quota Economics:
Without Caching (Naive)
10,000 readers in English = 10,000 Gemini API calls. Rate limits fail in 15 minutes; high SaaS fees.
With PublicSpark 4-Tier Caching
10,000 readers in English = Exactly 1 API Call. Remaining 9,999 visits served from 0ms LocalStorage or 7ms Disk Cache.
